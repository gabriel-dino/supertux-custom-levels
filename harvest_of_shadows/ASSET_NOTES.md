# Asset provenance

Creature, candy, cauldron, and lantern animation frames are retained from the original generated artwork. Halloween terrain, roads, backgrounds, music, and scenery are referenced from the installed game.

Only runtime assets needed by this pack are bundled. Full source atlases and the custom engine are not required to play and are not included. Referenced SuperTux artwork and music keep their upstream credits and licenses; the game supplies those files.

The original generated creature/prop PNGs and their original `.sprite` definitions bundled in this pack are designated **CC0-1.0**, to the extent the project holds rights in them. This also covers the transparent value-coin helpers when present. See `LICENSE-CC0-1.0.txt`.
