# Asset provenance

The five custom creature animations are retained from the original project. Their behavior is implemented by the bundled portable Squirrel controller.

Only runtime assets needed by this pack are bundled. Full source atlases and the custom engine are not required to play and are not included. Referenced SuperTux artwork and music keep their upstream credits and licenses; the game supplies those files.
