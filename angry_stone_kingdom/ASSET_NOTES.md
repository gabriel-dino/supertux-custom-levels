# Asset provenance

Imported on 2026-11-01 from the campaign under `/home/vecna/Downloads/supertux-master/data/levels/angry_stone_kingdom`. The source installation is unchanged.

- Original level layouts, numbered map nodes, map background and king HUD icon preserve the source campaign's CC0-1.0 dedication and author credit.
- `assets/images/angry_stone_kingdom/angrystone.sprite` and its three PNG frames preserve the original SuperTux Angry Stone graphics, copied unchanged into an add-on-specific namespace. These are upstream assets, excluded from CC0.
- `assets/images/angry_stone_kingdom/crown.png` is copied unchanged from the other portable world's original CC0 Marshmallow Monarch crown artwork, as reused by The Lost Plant World.
- Worldmap sprite definitions refer to stock SuperTux graphics. The worldmap tileset is adapted from the source definition and gives each stop exact road directions; these definitions and the Squirrel controller are GPL-3.0-or-later.
- All other referenced tiles, backgrounds, sounds, music and native enemy sprites are provided by standard SuperTux 0.7.0 and keep their upstream licenses. `ASSETS.json` in the development source records bundled hashes and stock paths.

The controller adapts AngryStone (Copyright 2006 Matthias Braun), the source campaign's KingAngryStone state machine and this project's portable-controller approach. `HISTORY.md` preserves the original historical research; its engine file references do not imply that engine files are shipped in this add-on.
